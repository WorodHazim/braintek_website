# Content governance

- Public positioning uses the three-pillar model: Cybersecurity & Digital Protection; Software Solutions & Systems Development; Manpower Development & Workforce Empowerment.
- Do not publish unverified metrics, client outcomes, or deployment claims.
- SentinelShield is a proposed solution concept unless BRAINTEK formally changes its status.
- OpsPilot is a strategic/configurable platform direction unless BRAINTEK formally confirms production maturity.
- The initial team visual treatment is non-photographic until real portraits are supplied.
- Partner descriptions preserve the evidential strength of the supplied documents; potential/possible collaboration must not be rewritten as completed work.
